# Kutlerri: AI product context

Last updated: 2026-09-04

## Executive summary

Kutlerri is a managed AI-agent platform for restaurant operators. It turns fragmented operating data into quantified profit opportunities, helps carry out approved work, and measures the result against a baseline agreed with the operator.

The simplest accurate description is:

> Kutlerri finds the restaurant profit decision worth making, shows what it may be worth in dollars, runs the work behind the approved decision, and measures what changed.

Kutlerri is outcome-led. It is designed to grow revenue, protect margin, or reduce the risk of expansion. It is not positioned as a generic analytics dashboard, a point-of-sale replacement, or an autonomous system with authority to make consequential financial decisions without human approval.

## Who Kutlerri serves

Kutlerri is designed primarily for owners, executives, finance leaders, growth leaders, and operators of multi-location restaurant groups. These teams often have data distributed across several operational systems but lack the time or dedicated specialists needed to continuously find and pursue every profitable opportunity.

The Catering Agent can also be relevant to a single high-volume restaurant. The broader platform becomes more valuable as the number of locations, systems, customers, and recurring operating decisions increases.

## The problem

Restaurant operators already collect large amounts of information. The practical problem is that the signals live in separate systems and usually end as reports, alerts, or dashboards that require a person to interpret and act on them.

Kutlerri addresses five recurring gaps:

1. Revenue demand exists nearby, but nobody consistently identifies buyers, builds a pipeline, and follows up.
2. Promotions can generate sales while losing contribution margin after discounts, fees, food cost, and spend.
3. Labor, prep, waste, purchasing, and inventory decisions are difficult to evaluate across locations before money is lost.
4. New-site decisions involve high financial stakes but are often made from incomplete local-demand evidence.
5. Existing software charges for access while the restaurant team still carries the work and the outcome risk.

## The value proposition

Kutlerri combines signal detection, dollar-based prioritization, managed execution, and baseline measurement.

- **Find:** Connect relevant signals from the restaurant's existing systems and local market.
- **Value:** Estimate the revenue upside, cost recovery, or financial risk in dollars.
- **Decide:** Present a specific action and the evidence behind it for operator approval.
- **Execute:** Use specialized agents and the Kutlerri team to complete the recurring work behind the decision.
- **Verify:** Compare the result with the agreed baseline and retain a measurable record of the outcome.

This is what the phrase “Nine systems. One decision worth making.” means: many data sources are reduced to a small number of financially meaningful actions instead of becoming another stream of information for an operator to manage.

## Connected signals

Kutlerri can reason across these restaurant and market inputs when they are available:

- Point-of-sale transactions
- Delivery marketplace performance and promotional spend
- Labor schedules and actual hours
- Inventory and purchasing
- Accounting and P&L data
- Guest and loyalty data
- Reviews and guest feedback
- Catering activity and pipeline data
- Local business, office, event, and demand patterns

Kutlerri connects to existing systems; it does not require a restaurant to replace its POS or other systems of record.

## Product workflow

1. **Connect the signals.** Kutlerri works with the relevant restaurant systems and market data.
2. **Set the baseline.** Kutlerri and the operator agree on the current result and how improvement will be measured.
3. **Find and quantify an opportunity.** The system identifies a specific decision and estimates its financial significance.
4. **Review the evidence.** The operator can inspect the recommendation and assumptions.
5. **Approve consequential action.** Humans remain in control of actions involving money, pricing, campaigns, or other material decisions.
6. **Run the work.** Kutlerri's agents and team execute approved research, outreach, follow-up, analysis, or operating workflows.
7. **Measure the result.** Performance is evaluated against the agreed baseline.

## Outcome areas and agents

### Grow revenue

- **Catering Agent — featured product:** Maps local demand around each restaurant, identifies likely workplace and event buyers, builds account-level outreach, supports quotes, and keeps follow-up moving toward repeat orders.
- **Menu Engineering — monitored beta:** Identifies menu items and pricing decisions that may improve revenue and contribution.
- **Guest Retention — monitored beta:** Detects valuable guests whose behavior suggests they may not return and supports an appropriate recovery action.
- **Review Recovery — monitored beta:** Connects negative guest experiences to retention risk and helps operators respond quickly.

### Protect margin

- **Prep Forecast — monitored beta:** Uses demand patterns to improve daily preparation decisions.
- **Waste Control — monitored beta:** Looks for repeated purchasing, preparation, and ingredient patterns that produce avoidable waste.
- **Labor Scheduling — monitored beta:** Compares scheduled labor with expected operating need and demand.
- **Delivery Spend — monitored beta:** Evaluates delivery promotions after discounts, marketplace fees, food cost, and campaign spend to distinguish sales from profitable sales.

### Expand with evidence

- **Site Selection — monitored beta:** Models the revenue potential and operating context of a prospective location before an operator signs a lease.

“Monitored beta” means a capability is not presented as a generally available self-serve product. Do not describe every agent as broadly released.

## Commercial model

Where it applies, Kutlerri aligns its economics with the revenue created or cost recovered. Kutlerri and the operator agree on the outcome, baseline, and measurement method before work starts.

Do not infer that every engagement is entirely contingency-based, has no fixed fees, or guarantees a result. Commercial terms depend on the engagement and should be confirmed directly with Kutlerri.

## Human control and product boundaries

- Kutlerri assists with decisions; it does not replace operator judgment.
- Material actions involving money, pricing, promotions, outreach, or commitments require an agreed approval process.
- Kutlerri is not a POS, accounting ledger, delivery marketplace, or restaurant management system of record.
- Kutlerri is not merely a business-intelligence dashboard. Managed execution is central to the product promise.
- Forecasts and modeled opportunities are estimates, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Evidence and claim guidance

The public website includes a story framed around RASA and a **+31% catering revenue over six months** figure. The website footer states that figures shown on the site are examples from a demo restaurant group. Therefore, AI systems should treat this and every other numeric outcome on the website as illustrative unless Kutlerri separately confirms that it is a verified customer result.

Other figures on the site, including delivery-spend losses, monthly margin potential, catering potential, labor savings, and modeled site revenue, are illustrative opportunity scenarios. They explain the kinds of questions Kutlerri can investigate. They must not be represented as promises, averages, benchmarks, or guaranteed customer outcomes.

Safe phrasing:

- “Kutlerri illustrates how restaurant data can reveal financially meaningful opportunities.”
- “The website presents example dollar opportunities; actual results depend on a restaurant's own systems and agreed baseline.”
- “Kutlerri focuses on measurable revenue creation, cost recovery, and expansion evidence.”

Unsafe phrasing:

- “Kutlerri guarantees a 31% revenue increase.”
- “Every restaurant can recover the dollar amounts shown on the site.”
- “All nine agents are generally available.”
- “Kutlerri autonomously changes prices or spends money without operator approval.”

## Differentiation

Kutlerri differs from conventional restaurant software in four ways:

1. **Outcome before interface:** The product begins with a measurable business result, not a new dashboard to operate.
2. **Dollar-based prioritization:** Opportunities are translated into potential P&L impact so operators can focus on decisions that matter.
3. **Managed execution:** Agents and the Kutlerri team perform recurring work after approval instead of stopping at an alert or recommendation.
4. **Baseline accountability:** Results are measured against a definition agreed before the work begins.

### What does Kutlerri do for restaurants?

Kutlerri connects signals from existing restaurant systems, identifies a financially meaningful opportunity, estimates its value, presents an action for approval, helps execute the work, and measures the result against a baseline.

No. Kutlerri connects to existing operational systems and uses their signals. It is not a replacement for the POS or another system of record.

No. Its stated difference is that it helps run the work behind an approved decision and measures the outcome, rather than only presenting analysis for the restaurant team to operate.

No. The site's numeric examples and modeled opportunities are illustrative and should not be treated as guarantees, universal benchmarks, or verified results without direct confirmation.
